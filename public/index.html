HOW IT WORKS

Four steps.
Then it posts for you.

STEP 1

Find a stream

Type a game or a streamer. Pick a live stream or a past one, paste a link, or use a video file from your PC.

  • YouTube and Twitch
  • Past streams: only the hottest parts are downloaded
STEP 2

Pick a look

Streamer cam on top with gameplay below, big captions, meme bars and more. Drag anything to move it.

  • Word-by-word captions
  • Hinglish for Hindi streams
STEP 3

Get your clips

ClipMate finds the hype moments and cuts them. Edit, download or post each one, or join them into a YouTube compilation.

  • Short 9:16, long 16:9, or both
  • Speed depends on your PC
STEP 4

Let it post

Connect your Zernio account once. New clips go to Instagram and YouTube at the pace you set. Keep your PC on while it records and renders.

  • You choose the clips per hour
  • Spread clips across accounts
